Slicefall: Not Tetris 2 gameplay in your browser

Slicefall is a free web port of the gameplay from Not Tetris 2 by Maurice Guégan. Falling blocks are real rigid bodies: you push and spin them, they tumble and stack, and once a row holds enough area it is sliced straight through the pieces. No download or install.

Modes: Cut (endless, with weekly and all-time leaderboards), Versus (two players on one keyboard or two gamepads) and Online (two players on their own devices, joined by a code). Plays with keyboard, gamepad or touch controls on phones and tablets.

Not Tetris 2 is a physics take on Tetris, created by Alexey Pajitnov in 1984. Tetris is a trademark of The Tetris Company; this game is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.
